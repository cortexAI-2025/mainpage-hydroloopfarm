Why HydroLoop

Vertical farms grow upward. We also grow forward.

Conventional vertical NFT systems stack plants but treat them as a single crop. HydroLoop was designed around the fact that every stage of a plant's life needs something different.

01 — NUTRITION

One tank for every stage

A single shared solution makes it impossible to tune pH, EC and nutrient balance to each cohort.

→ One sealed loop per level, no backflow.

02 — LIGHT

Upper levels shade lower ones

Stacked shelves leave the bottom rows in the dark and the harvest uneven.

→ Staggered A-Frame, 10 cm anti-shading offset.

03 — CADENCE

Harvest, then wait

Batch systems empty, clean and replant — weeks with no output and irregular cash flow.

→ Calibrated cohort rotation, continuous output.

04 — RECORDS

Paper-based traceability

Regulated buyers need batch-level records that most farms can't produce reliably.

→ A digital passport for every cohort.

The module

Three levels. Three loops. One frame.

A self-supporting A-Frame in galvanised steel or anodised aluminium carries three levels of food-grade PVC NFT channels. Each level is its own growing chamber, with a dedicated reservoir, pump and aeration.

N1

Vegetative growth

Young plants arrive from the nursery at day 10 and build roots and leaves in the bottom loop.

200 mm channelEC 1.2–1.6 mS/cm100 L tank
N2

Stretch & pre-flowering

Plants elongate and transition, with a richer solution and more space — in a loop of their own.

300 mm channelEC 1.8–2.2 mS/cm50 L tank
N3

Flowering & maturation

Mature plants take the top position, closest to the light, until harvest.

400 mm channelEC 2.4–2.8 mS/cm50 L tank
2%

A film, not a flood

Channels run at a 2% slope (40 mm over 2 m) for a laminar nutrient film of 1–3 mm. No soil, no substrate: roots sit in a recirculating film, so water is reused instead of drained.

Continuous rotation

A cohort moves up. Another one comes in.

Plants don't stay put. At every interval, each cohort climbs one level, the top cohort is harvested and a fresh one enters at the bottom. The module is never empty — and never idle.

  1. DAY 0 – 9Germination

    In a separate nursery, outside the module.

  2. DAY 10Level 1 — growth

    A cohort of 16 plants per level joins the bottom loop.

  3. +20 to 27 DAYSLevel 2 — stretch

    Moved up; the solution and spacing change with it.

  4. +20 to 27 DAYSLevel 3 — flowering

    Top position, strongest light, maturation profile.

  5. HARVESTHarvest, clean, re-enter

    Fresh-frozen at −40 °C, level cleaned in place, next cohort moves up.

The companion app

Every batch, followed from seed to harvest.

Each module comes with the HydroLoop Farm Manager, a web app developed by AIWorkPay that installs on any phone. Growers log a batch in a few taps, see what needs attention and get a clear report on what to adjust.

Batch ID, cultivar, sowing date, module, phase and day — plus pH, EC, solution temperature, dissolved oxygen, NFT flow, PPFD and photoperiod.

Instant alerts

Flags readings outside the safe range: pH below 5.5 or above 6.5, dissolved O₂ under 6 mg/L, solution above 22 °C, low NFT flow.

AI agronomy report

A structured analysis of roots, leaves, trims and flowers for each batch, with system inefficiencies and recommendations you can act on today.

Harvest prediction

Estimated harvest date and window, with a confidence level and what to check on the plants as the date gets closer.

QR code per batch

Print a label for each cohort. Scanning it opens the batch record — the base of the Taqnin ID Batch passport.

Analytics & comparisons

Batches by cultivar, alert frequency, phase distribution, environmental averages and side-by-side comparisons of the same cultivar.

Works offline

Installs like a native app and keeps working in the greenhouse without signal. Records sync to the cloud when the connection returns.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is HydroLoop Farm?

HydroLoop Farm is a modular vertical hydroponic system designed in Morocco. A self-supporting A-Frame (2.0 × 2.0 × 1.5 m) carries three levels of NFT channels, each with its own independent nutrient loop, and plants move up one level every 20–27 days so the module harvests continuously.

Who designed HydroLoop Farm and who holds the patent?

HydroLoop Farm was designed by Ahmed Akafou, who is also the patent holder. The patent application was filed with OMPIC (Morocco), with a PCT international extension planned.

How many plants does one module hold?

48 plants per module: 16 plants on each of the three levels.

How often does a module harvest?

Every 20 to 27 days depending on the cultivar, which is 13.5 to 18.25 harvests per year, with no idle period between cycles.

How much water does it save?

More than 80% compared with soil-based growing, because the nutrient solution recirculates in closed loops with no drainage.

What is the difference between HydroLoop Farm and HydroLoop Inside?

Both share the same frame, loops and rotation. HydroLoop Farm runs in a heated greenhouse on natural sunlight with automatic LED top-up; HydroLoop Inside runs in an opaque room or container with full-spectrum LED differentiated per level and sensors for temperature, humidity, CO₂, pH and EC.

What does the companion app do?

The HydroLoop Farm Manager, developed by AIWorkPay, logs each batch from seed to harvest, raises alerts on pH, EC, dissolved oxygen, solution temperature and NFT flow, produces an AI agronomy report, predicts the harvest window, generates a QR code per batch, shows analytics and works offline with cloud sync. It is available at https://app.hydroloopfarm.com.

How is traceability handled?

Each cohort receives a unique Taqnin ID Batch identifier recording genetics, operators, dates, environmental readings, harvest weight, freezing curve and destination, aligned with GACP, pre-GMP, ANRAC (Law 13-21) and ONSSA requirements.
